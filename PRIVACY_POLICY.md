# Privacy Policy

**Bae Talk** operates the Bae Talk mobile and web application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection & Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:
- **Personal Data**: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"), including your Email address, Full name, Phone number, and Shipping Address.
- **Cart & Wishlist Data**: We save your shopping selections (items, sizes, colors) to provide order checkout and product tracking. In offline demo mode, this data is saved locally on your device's secure storage.

## 2. Authentication & Third-Party Services

We utilize secure, industry-standard authentication methods:
- **Firebase Auth**: To secure your email/password sign-in.
- **Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple**: For quick, secure login integrations.
- **Razorpay**: To securely process payments without storing credit card details on our servers.

We do not sell, trade, or rent users' personal identification information to others.

## 3. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

To improve application stability, we collect diagnostic logs and crash signatures using **Firebase Crashlytics**. This data does not contain personally identifiable information and is used solely to fix crashes and optimize performance.
